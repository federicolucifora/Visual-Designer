Le Auto della Dolce Vita
3D Modeling | Texturing | Retopology | Baking
Le Auto della Dolce Vita is a project commissioned by the Automobile Club Italia. My main contribution was the creation of the Triumph, a vintage sports car: I started from a pre-existing asset of a similar car and used reference images and photogrammetry to refine it into an accurate representation. I also created the environment in which the car is placed, the Trevi Fountain, retopologizing and retexturing a pre-existing model. Both were designed for the Apple Vision Pro, aiming for near-photorealistic results, and consist of approximately 1 million triangles each.
The project was also promoted on social media through motion graphics videos. I personally worked on the transition from a wireframe digital environment to a fully textured one.

The DNA of Rome
Reality Composer Pro | Infographic Design | Projection Mapping
The DNA of Rome is an installation created for Roma nel Mondo at MAXXI Museum, where light and data are projected onto a monumental terracotta model of the city, turning it into a living map of Rome's history and infrastructure.
My work on the project centered on Reality Composer Pro, where I built the spatial experience ahead of installation.

I was also responsible for managing the infographics, making sure each one matched the terracotta model's surface and geometry so the projections stayed precisely aligned during the show.
